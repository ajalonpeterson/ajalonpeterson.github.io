remote sensing, cartographic automation
Mapping Change Around Qatar's World Cup Stadiums
Differenced Sentinel-2 imagery from 2016, 2022, and 2025 to map visible change at each World Cup stadium, then built a single ArcGIS Pro map series that labels, re-centers, and exports all seven stadiums automatically in one run.

site suitability, renewable energy
Ranking Minnesota's Best Sites for Utility-Scale Solar
A weighted GIS model combining land cover, parcel size, slope, aspect, and road proximity to rank candidate solar sites statewide — with real limitations named directly rather than smoothed over.
ArcPyMulti-criteria analysis
OCR, historical maps
Predicting Modern Locations from a 1930s Plat Map
A scanned plat has no coordinates; just hand-lettered street names running at every angle. This pipeline masks out the noise with a GIS buffering technique, pulls the street names with OCR, then matches and clusters them against today's street network to reverse-geocode the plat onto an actual, present-day address.

About

I'm a recent graduate of the University of Minnesota, with a B.S. in Geography (GIS) and Plant Science. I was introduced to GIS through a precision agriculture course, and loved it so much that I added it as a second major.

My background spans both plant science and GIS, which gives me a unique toolset for tackling large-scale spatial problems with real scientific grounding behind them: not just the geometry, but why it matters ecologically.

Outside of work I'm usually spending time enjoying what sparked my interest in this field in the first place, the great outdoors. Hiking, kayaking, and plant identification along the way make me feel more in tune with the landscape, and with myself.
